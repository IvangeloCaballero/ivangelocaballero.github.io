Modular Filtration Skid - Mechanical Layout Design

Piping system layout and flow design.

Overview

This project involves the design of a modular filtration skid, focusing on piping layout, flow routing, and component arrangement. The system integrates process functionality with practical considerations such as accessibility, structural support, and material estimation.

System Layout

Multi-view representation of the system showing orthographic and isometric perspectives for spatial understanding.

Process Flow and Layout

The process flow diagram illustrates the sequence of operation from water inlet through pumping, filtration, and distribution to outlet streams. Flow paths are arranged to maintain continuity while allowing parallel filtration and separate instrumentation discharge. The floor plan shows how these components are spatially arranged within the skid, ensuring efficient routing, accessibility, and compact system integration.

Component Design and Standard Integration

The system incorporates both custom-designed components and standard parts. Valves and piping elements were selected from Inventor Content Center to ensure compatibility with commonly used industrial components. Major equipment such as the filtration unit, storage tanks, pump configuration, and supporting skid structure were designed and modeled to meet system requirements and spatial constraints.

Flange connections were detailed to reflect proper mating conditions and sealing interfaces, following standard dimensional practices aligned with ASME B16.5.

  • Custom modeling of tanks, filter unit, pump setup, and skid frame
  • Use of Inventor Content Center for standard valves and piping
  • Flange design aligned with ASME B16.5 dimensions
  • Consideration of alignment, sealing surfaces, and bolt arrangement

Flange section detailing mating geometry and sealing interface.

A bill of materials was developed to document the major components of the system and provide an initial cost estimate. The breakdown includes primary equipment, piping elements, and fittings, supporting evaluation of material requirements and overall project feasibility.
