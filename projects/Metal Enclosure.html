Sheet‑Metal Electrical Enclosure - Design for Manufacturability

Fabrication‑ready sheet‑metal design

Design Summary

Modular sheet‑metal electrical enclosure developed as a fabrication‑focused design study, emphasizing ventilation strategy and service accessibility with representative production‑intent documentation.

Key Design Considerations

  • Panel layout and bend geometry were designed to simplify fabrication and reduce part complexity.
  • Bend allowance was defined using a K-factor of approximately 0.34, consistent with mild steel sheet metal forming.
  • Fastener locations were positioned to allow accessible assembly and maintenance.
  • Sheet thickness (1.5 mm) was selected based on structural requirements while maintaining compatibility with standard fabrication processes.
  • Clearances between mating panels and door interfaces were maintained at approximately 1 mm to ensure proper fit and alignment.
  • Ventilation openings were incorporated to allow airflow while maintaining structural rigidity of the enclosure.

Modular Architecture & Assembly Intent

Exploded view illustrating how the enclosure is decomposed into independently fabricated panels and access components.

Key Components & Functional Role
  • A — Main Structural Chassis
    Folded sheet‑metal shell providing structural support, mounting features, and identification provisions.
  • B — Ventilation Side Panel (Exhaust / Outlet)
    Ventilated side panel configured to support air exhaust and define the enclosure airflow path.
  • C — Ventilation Side Panel (Passive Intake)
    Removable side panel with integrated openings for passive air intake.
  • D — Front Access Door Assembly
    Hinged door with gasket and quarter‑turn latch for sealed, tool‑free service access.
