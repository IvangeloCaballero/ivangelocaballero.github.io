Sheet‑Metal Electrical Enclosure - Fabrication Design

Design for manufacturability with focus on fabrication efficiency and serviceability.

Overview

Modular sheet‑metal electrical enclosure developed as a fabrication‑focused design study, emphasizing ventilation strategy and service accessibility with representative production‑intent documentation.

Key Design Considerations

  • Panel layout and bend geometry were designed to simplify fabrication and reduce part complexity.
  • Bend allowance was defined using a K-factor of approximately 0.34, consistent with mild steel forming.
  • Fastener locations were positioned for accessible assembly and maintenance.
  • Sheet thickness (1.5 mm) was selected to balance structural strength and manufacturability.
  • Clearances between mating panels (~1 mm) ensure proper fit and alignment.
  • Ventilation openings were incorporated to maintain airflow while preserving structural rigidity.

Modular Architecture & Assembly Intent

Exploded view illustrating how the enclosure is decomposed into independently fabricated panels and service components.

Key Components & Functional Role
  • A — Main Structural Chassis
    Folded sheet‑metal shell providing structural support and mounting features.
  • B — Ventilation Side Panel (Exhaust)
    Panel configured for directional airflow and heat dissipation.
  • C — Ventilation Side Panel (Intake)
    Removable panel supporting passive airflow intake.
  • D — Front Access Door Assembly
    Hinged door with latch for secure and accessible servicing.
