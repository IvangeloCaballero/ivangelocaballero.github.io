Sheet‑Metal Electrical Enclosure - Fabrication Design

Design for manufacturability with focus on fabrication efficiency and serviceability.

Overview

Modular sheet‑metal electrical enclosure developed as a fabrication‑focused design study, emphasizing ventilation strategy and service accessibility with representative production‑intent documentation.

Key Design Considerations

  • A material thickness of 1.5 mm was selected to provide adequate enclosure rigidity while remaining compatible with common laser cutting and press-brake forming processes.
  • An internal bend radius of 1.5 mm was applied throughout the design to maintain forming consistency and reduce localized material stresses during bending operations.
  • A K-factor of 0.34 was used for flat-pattern development, providing realistic bend allowance calculations for mild-steel sheet metal and improving dimensional accuracy after forming.
  • Return flanges were incorporated to increase panel stiffness and minimize deflection without increasing material thickness.
  • Panel clearances of approximately 1 mm were maintained to ensure proper fit-up while accommodating manufacturing tolerances and assembly requirements.
  • Ventilation louvers were positioned to encourage passive airflow while preserving structural integrity and simplifying fabrication.

Modular Architecture & Assembly Intent

Exploded view illustrating how the enclosure is decomposed into independently fabricated panels, sealing components, and standard hardware to simplify manufacturing, assembly, and maintenance.

Component Overview

Component Functional Role Manufacturing Method
A — Main Structural Chassis Primary enclosure structure providing rigidity, mounting interfaces, and support for all attached components. Laser Cutting → Press Brake Forming → Powder Coating
B — Ventilation Side Panel (Exhaust) Provides exhaust airflow while protecting internal components and maintaining enclosure integrity. Laser Cutting → Louver Forming → Press Brake Forming → Powder Coating
C — Ventilation Side Panel (Intake) Supports passive intake airflow while allowing easy panel removal for servicing and inspection. Laser Cutting → Louver Forming → Press Brake Forming → Powder Coating
D — Front Access Door Provides secure access to internal equipment for maintenance and operation. Laser Cutting → Press Brake Forming → Powder Coating
E — Door Gasket Creates a sealing interface between the door and enclosure frame. Die-Cut EPDM Rubber
F — Cam Latch Secures the access door during operation while allowing rapid servicing. Standard Purchased Component
G — Hinge Assembly Supports door rotation and enables repeatable opening and closing. Standard Purchased Component
