Sample Mechanical Enclosure

Placeholder project demonstrating layout for portfolio case studies.

Overview

This sample page outlines a compact electronics enclosure designed for sheet-metal fabrication and service access.

Problem / Objective

The objective was to protect internal components from dust and ESD while maintaining airflow for a small thermal load, within a fixed BOM cost.

Design Process

Requirements were translated into a concept set; the down-selected concept was detailed in CAD with attention to bend radii, fastener access, and cable routing.

  • Stakeholder requirements & risk review
  • Concept sketches & CAD exploration
  • Prototype feedback loop

Tools Used

  • CAD: SolidWorks (placeholder—edit to your tools)
  • Rendering: KeyShot / native CAD
  • Documentation: drawings, BOM

Final Outcome

The outcome is a manufacturable design with defined tolerances and assembly order, ready for vendor quote and first article.
