About

I am a mechanical design engineer working on CAD‑based design, assemblies, and technical documentation for automotive and industrial systems. My focus is on producing clear, manufacturable outputs that can be reliably used across engineering teams, suppliers, and production environments.

My work is detail‑driven and execution‑focused, with emphasis on design clarity, drawing control, and structured engineering data. I prioritize outputs that are consistent, complete, and ready for manufacturing use.
