About

I am a mechanical design engineer working on CAD‑based mechanical design, assemblies, drawings, and engineering documentation for automotive and industrial systems. My focus is on producing clear, manufacturable outputs that can be used reliably across engineering teams, suppliers, and production environments.

My work is detail‑driven and execution‑focused, with emphasis on drawing control, managed design data, and legacy design updates. I prioritize completeness, consistency, and clear documentation to support reliable engineering handoff and manufacturing use.
